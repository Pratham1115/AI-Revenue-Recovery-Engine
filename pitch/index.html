Compliance & Safety

Trust is hard-coded,
not an afterthought

🌙 Quiet Hours Enforcement

No voice/SMS/WhatsApp between 21:00–09:00 customer local time. Scheduler recalculates against IANA timezone DB before every dispatch.

⚡ Circuit Breaker (<500ms)

Real-time dispute/chargeback webhook terminates ALL active state machines for that customer within 500ms. Zero manual intervention required.

📏 Touchpoint Limits

Maximum 3 touchpoints per 7 days per customer, across all channels. Hard-coded — cannot be overridden by config.

💬 Tone Monitor

All AI-generated messages validated against FDCPA / RBI Digital Recovery Guidelines. No aggressive language. All voice calls include mandatory disclosure statement.

🎁 Discount Authority Ceiling

Max 5% recovery incentive only if customer LTV > ₹2.5L AND churn risk > 85%. Validated against user role authorization before sending.

🛑 DNC Respect

Instant opt-out on "DO NOT CALL" response. Stops all channels immediately. Persisted permanently — never re-activated without explicit consent.

Module 4 — Attribution Engine

We prove the lift.
No credit-stealing.

Net_Agent_Recovery =
Total_Cohort_Recovered
− (Control_Rate × Treatment_Total)

= Proven incremental dollars, not vanity metric

5% Holdout Group

Every 20th event (determined by MD5 hash of recovery_id) is routed to control — no intervention. Their organic recovery rate becomes the baseline.

72-Hour Attribution Window

Recovery within 72h of last intervention = AGENT_DRIVEN.
Recovery after window = ORGANIC_BASELINE.

LIVE LEDGER STATUSES

AGENT_DRIVEN

Customer recovered within 72h of AI intervention. Verified lift above baseline.

ORGANIC_BASELINE

Customer paid independently. Correctly excluded from agent recovery credit.

HOLDOUT

Control group — no intervention. Measures true organic baseline for lift calculation.
